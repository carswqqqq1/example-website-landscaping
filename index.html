How It Works

From First Walkthrough to Finished Yard

01

Initial Project Review

Typically 60-90 minutes on-site. We visit your property, listen to your vision, understand your lifestyle and priorities, and outline what is realistic for your space.

02

Design & 3D Rendering

Most concept planning is mapped within 1-2 weeks depending on scope. Our designers produce a detailed landscape plan, including 3D renderings when the project calls for them.

03

Expert Construction

Build timing depends on scope, but we give a clear schedule before work starts. Our team executes the project with precision and handles trade coordination without the usual handoff issues.

04

Walkthrough & Warranty

Final walkthrough happens before closeout so details can be reviewed together. We address finishing items, explain care expectations, and document the workmanship warranty.
