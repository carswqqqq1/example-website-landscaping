Decide these before choosing pavers

  • Patio size

    Size the patio around the table and chairs you actually own, plus room to walk.

  • Drainage

    Slope water away from the house and toward planting, not the neighbor.

  • Material

    Pavers cost more than concrete up front but repair one piece at a time.
