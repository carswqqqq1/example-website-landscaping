Outdoor kitchens

Grill, prep and seating laid out around how you cook, with gas, power and shade planned up front.

Plan my outdoor kitchen See example work

What you can plan

  • Custom kitchen layout and appliance planning
  • Countertop and cabinet finish coordination
  • Integrated prep, serving, and seating zones
  • Utility routing support for power, gas, and water
  • Heat-ready material recommendations
  • Design continuity with surrounding hardscape

What affects the scope

  • Appliance package, utility routing, island size, counter material, and cabinet or masonry style.
  • Whether the kitchen needs adjacent patio work, shade, lighting, seating, or fire-feature coordination.
  • Gas, electric, water, drainage, access, and finish choices that affect labor and schedule.

Typical planning range

$20k to $60k

For a typical Valley yard. Size, access and finish choices move it up or down.
