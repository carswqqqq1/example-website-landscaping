Patios, walkways and stonework

Pavers and stone laid on a proper base, with drainage and edges planned for Arizona heat.

Plan my hardscape See example work

What you can plan

  • Paver patio and walkway installation
  • Retaining walls and seating walls with clean detailing
  • Step systems, transitions, and grade corrections
  • Edging, border control, and material integration
  • Drainage-aware base prep and slope planning
  • Finish options matched to your home style

What affects the scope

  • Square footage, demo, base depth, drainage correction, edge restraint, and finish material.
  • Transitions into doors, steps, walls, turf, planting, fire features, kitchens, or existing concrete.
  • Access, haul-off, grade changes, compaction needs, and whether the patio supports future phases.

Typical planning range

$10k to $40k

For a typical Valley yard. Size, access and finish choices move it up or down.
