Irrigation repair and new systems

Fix dry spots, leaks and runoff, or plan drip and zones for new planting.

Fix an irrigation problem Plan a new system See example work

What you can plan

  • Drip and spray zone planning by plant type
  • Controller programming and optimization
  • Coverage checks and pressure-aware layout
  • Water-efficiency upgrades for existing systems
  • Leak and runtime issue troubleshooting
  • Irrigation integration with new landscape installs

What affects the scope

  • Zone count, valve access, controller needs, water pressure, drip conversion, and system condition.
  • Whether the scope is troubleshooting, retrofit, new planting support, or full system redesign.
  • Plant types, sun exposure, runoff, leaks, trenching access, and compatibility with future landscape work.

Typical planning range

$2k to $10k

For a typical Valley yard. Size, access and finish choices move it up or down.
