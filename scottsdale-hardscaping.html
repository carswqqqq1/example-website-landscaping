Decide these before choosing pavers

  • Surface heat

    Lighter travertine and pavers stay cooler underfoot around pools than dark concrete.

  • Drainage

    Slope and edge details matter more than the paver itself when summer storms hit.

  • Future features

    Leave sleeves for gas, power or lighting if a fire pit or kitchen may come later.
