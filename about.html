About Mesa Wren

One team from design through construction

Mesa Wren plans and builds residential landscapes across the Phoenix Valley. The people who draw the plan are the people who build it.

How design-build works here

You get one plan for layout, planting, shade and lighting, then one team that prices and builds it. When something changes on site, it is sorted out inside the team and you hear what it means for the plan, not who is to blame.

  • Plan first. Layout and materials are settled before anything is priced as a build.
  • Scope in writing. What is included, what is not and how changes work are written down before work starts.
  • Materials for the heat. Paving, plants and drip systems are picked for summer sun, monsoon rain and local soil.
  • A weekly update. During a build you hear what happens this week, what comes next and what is needed from you.

Who it suits

Homeowners who want a finished yard without managing a designer, a contractor and suppliers separately. That includes a focused first phase, like turf and a patio, with room to add shade, lighting or a kitchen later.

Why Arizona yards need their own plan

Hard caliche under the soil, monsoon runoff and HOA plant lists all change what works. Planning for them up front is cheaper than fixing a patio that cracked or a bed that flooded.
