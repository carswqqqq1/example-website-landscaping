Our Story

Built in Arizona, For Arizona Yards

Mesa Wren started with a simple observation: most landscaping in the Phoenix Valley was designed for somewhere else. Plants that couldn't handle 115-degree summers. Drainage that failed the first monsoon. Hardscape that cracked because no one accounted for how the ground moves here.

We plan around how Arizona yards actually behave: the caliche layers in North Scottsdale that need proper excavation, the flood irrigation schedules in Arcadia, the HOA palettes across Desert Mountain and Troon North, and the grade changes in Cave Creek that need real drainage before a single paver goes down.

We started because homeowners kept getting handed off between designers, contractors and suppliers who never talked to each other, and the yard paid for it.

How We Work

What Sets Us Apart

  • Design-Build Accountability

    Planning, material selections, scheduling and construction stay coordinated through one team. When something needs to change, we handle it internally and update you, so you're never stuck between a designer and a contractor blaming each other.

  • Arizona-Specific Material Planning

    We specify travertine, pavers, flagstone, desert-adapted plants, drip and lighting for how they perform in Phoenix heat, monsoon rain and Arizona soil, not how they photograph in a catalog. What looks good at install still needs to look good in five years.

  • Scope Clarity Before Anything Starts

    No build starts without a signed agreement that defines the scope, materials, exclusions and change-order process. You know exactly what's included before the first stake goes in the ground.

  • Homeowner Communication Throughout

    During every active build you hear what's happening this week, what's next, and what we need from you before we can proceed. No silence and no guessing when your yard will be usable again.
