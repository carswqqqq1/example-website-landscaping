Process

How a Mesa Wren project works

Four stages, from the first walk of your yard to the handoff, with what you see and what you decide at each one.

The four stages

  1. 1

    Phase 1: Walkthrough

    What you see
    A visit to walk the yard and talk through what you want to use, fix and keep.
    What we need from you
    Your goals, a rough budget comfort, HOA rules and anything about access, pets or gates.

  2. 2

    Phase 2: Direction

    What you see
    A plan, a planning range and a sensible first phase, with what waits for later.
    What we need from you
    Your picks on layout and materials, and which zone matters most.

  3. 3

    Phase 3: Build

    What you see
    One crew working to a written scope and a schedule you saw before work started, with a weekly update.
    What we need from you
    Site access on build days and quick answers when a decision comes up.

  4. 4

    Phase 4: Handoff

    What you see
    A walk of the finished yard together, care notes for plants and irrigation, and the warranty in writing.
    What we need from you
    Time for the final walk and a list of anything that does not look right.

Before a crew arrives

  • HOA approvals, material sheets and plant lists, if your community reviews them.
  • Gate codes, parking, pets, pool access and the hours crews can work.
  • Where materials are staged, how surfaces are protected and daily cleanup.
  • Changes are approved in writing before the crew does them.

Warranty details are on the warranty page.
