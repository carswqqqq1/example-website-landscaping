Decide these before choosing fixtures

  • Glare and dark skies

    Shielded, low-level fixtures light paths and trees without washing out the view.

  • Zones and timers

    Separate entry, patio and accent zones so each can be dimmed or scheduled.

  • Wiring before paving

    Running conduit before hardscape goes in avoids cutting new stone later.
