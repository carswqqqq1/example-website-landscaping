Decide these before choosing turf

  • Pets or play

    Pet areas need a draining infill and base; play areas may need extra padding.

  • Edges and borders

    Clean edges against pavers and planting beds are what make turf look finished.

  • Sun and heat

    Turf gets hot in full sun, so shade or nearby planting helps on summer afternoons.
