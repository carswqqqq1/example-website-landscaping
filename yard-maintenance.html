Pick a Schedule

Three simple plans, priced to your yard

Every plan includes the same core visit. The only difference is how often we come out.

Weekly

For turf lawns in peak season, high-traffic yards, and homes that host often. The yard never gets ahead of you.

Biweekly

The most popular plan in the Valley. Keeps beds, rock, and edges clean without paying for visits you do not need.

Monthly

For low-water desert yards and xeriscape. A full tidy, weed pass, and irrigation check once a month.

Every Visit

What a maintenance visit covers

  • Mowing and turf care

    Mowing, edging, and blowing for natural grass. Brushing and debris pickup for artificial turf.

  • Trimming and shaping

    Shrubs, ground cover, and low branches kept off walkways, walls, and the roofline.

  • Weed control

    Hand pulling and pre-emergent in beds and decomposed granite so weeds do not take over after the monsoon.

  • Irrigation check

    A quick look at drip lines, emitters, and the controller schedule so plants get water without waste.
