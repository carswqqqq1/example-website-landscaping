What affects the scope

  • Yard size, and how much is turf, rock, beds or hedges.
  • How often the crew comes, and the season: turf and weeds need more in summer and after the monsoon.
  • Extras beyond the core visit, like tree trimming, pre-emergent or irrigation repairs.

Every visit covers

Mowing or turf brushing, trimming off walkways and walls, weed control in beds and granite, and a quick drip and controller check.
