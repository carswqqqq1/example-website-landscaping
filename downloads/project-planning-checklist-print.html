Landscape Project Planning Checklist

Use this checklist before your project review to clarify scope, budget direction, timeline, and must-have features so the first project conversation is faster and more accurate.

1. Project Vision

  • What do you want your outdoor space to do better?
  • Prioritize 3 outcomes: entertaining, curb appeal, low maintenance, shade, family use.

2. Service Scope

  • Landscape Design & Build
  • Hardscaping
  • Outdoor Kitchen
  • Fire Features
  • Artificial Turf
  • Irrigation
  • Outdoor Lighting

3. Budget Direction

  • $10k-$25k refresh
  • $25k-$60k transformation
  • $60k+ luxury build

4. Timeline

  • Planning for later
  • 3-6 months
  • 1-3 months
  • Within 30 days
  • ASAP

5. Site Conditions

  • Drainage or pooling
  • Irrigation performance
  • Dead plant areas
  • Shade and sun challenges
  • Access constraints

6. Must-Have Features

  • Paver patio
  • Fire pit or fireplace
  • Outdoor kitchen
  • Turf or putting green
  • Lighting
  • Water feature

7. Inspiration References

  • Save 5-10 photos of styles you like.
  • Mark material preferences and color direction.

8. Project Review Notes

  • Write your top questions for the design team.
  • Keep HOA or property constraints ready if applicable.
